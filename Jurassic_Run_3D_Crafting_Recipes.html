JURASSIC RUN

Survival 3D · NIGHTMARE DINOSAURS
Explore a growing open world in every direction, discover the lava biome, collect coins, and survive dinosaur, tiger, and crocodile attacks!

❤️ 500 health · 8 shooting guns · SLOWER, LESS FREQUENT MOBS · ONE BOSS EVERY 10 MINUTES · LAVA BIOME · ONE-SHOT LAVA ROCK MONSTER · CRAFTABLE LAVA ARMOR

🌿 Optional soothing jungle ambience: tap “Jungle ambience” in the top-right for soft wind, a low calming drone, and gentle bird calls. Health bars appear above animals. Craft armor and equip it using bottom hotbar slots 1–5 (or number keys); only equipped armor reduces damage. Caves always contain terrifying cave dinosaurs with glowing red eyes, sharp teeth, horns, and creepy scars.

Computer: WASD move, mouse look, click shoot, Shift sprint, I opens your 100-slot inventory, E enter/exit nearby caves or open shop, F harvest trees for wood or Emberwood. Press C to open crafting recipes: crafting table (10 wood), furnace (14 wood), medkit (4 wood + 5 coins), wood/iron/gold/diamond/lava gear, and craftable guns using wood, cave ores, and coins. Build a crafting table before crafting guns. Press H to use a medkit. Rivers, mountains, ground holes, spike traps, and villages with stationary friendly villagers appear in the open world. Villages have a 90% chance to generate in eligible new world chunks and are kept at least about 58 meters from you when they spawn. Press E near a villager to see epic trades, then press T to buy; villagers offer weapons, diamonds, gold gear, healing, and rare ores. You always spawn in the starter village. Every village has 20 houses, one treasure chest inside each house, 10 crop farms, plus 3 extra village treasure chests. Chests can contain armor upgrades as well as huge loot: coins, wood, iron, gold, diamonds, and sometimes a free weapon unlock. Near a cave entrance, press E to explore underground caves. Each cave visit contains a powerful rock dinosaur and another cave dinosaur! Equip armor using the bottom hotbar slots 1–5 (or number keys); crafted armor does not protect you until equipped. Find iron, gold, and diamond ore deposits underground and press G close to a deposit to mine it. Explore freely in every direction in an expanding open world. The lava biome lies deeper in the jungle and contains a giant glowing-crack LAVA ROCK MONSTER. Its attack instantly kills you, even with armor, so keep your distance and shoot it from far away; after defeat it can return after 45 seconds. Gather 5 Emberwood, then buy lava armor for 80 coins in the weapon shop; it reduces damage by 60%. Press B to build a secure house for 35 wood/coins (you need 35 coins); dinosaurs cannot pass its walls or hurt you while you are inside. Survive waves that grow harder up to 1,000,000.
